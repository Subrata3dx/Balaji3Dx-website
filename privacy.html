Privacy at a glance

The Final Notes helps users store information, schedule wellbeing check-ins, communicate with trusted contacts, and share authorised information with their chosen nominees. 2. Scope

This Policy applies to The Final Notes website, application, related support services, email and WhatsApp communications, and services that link to this Policy. It does not govern third-party websites or services that publish their own privacy policies.

3. Personal data we collect

Depending on how the service is used, we may collect:

  • Account and identity information: name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, login and account information, country, time zone, and communication preferences.
  • Check-in information: check-in questions, schedules, reminders, responses, response times, account status, and escalation history.
  • Trusted-contact and nominee information: names, relationships, email addresses, telephone or WhatsApp numbers, postal addresses, communication preferences, consent status, and responses. Users must have authority to provide this information and should inform these individuals that their data has been supplied to us.
  • Stored notes and files: information, instructions, messages, documents, attachments, and other content a user chooses to store or direct us to share.
  • Communications: support requests, email messages, WhatsApp replies, delivery status, provider message identifiers, and related communication records.
  • Subscription and payment information: plan, subscription status, transaction references, payment status, and billing records. Payment-card or banking details may be collected directly by a payment processor rather than by us.
  • Technical and security information: IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, server logs, session information, authentication events, and security or audit records.

Please do not provide information that is unnecessary for the service. Because stored notes and files may contain sensitive information, users should carefully consider what they upload and who they designate to receive it.

4. How we obtain personal data

We receive data directly from users, from their interactions with the service, from people responding as trusted contacts or nominees, and from service providers that support hosting, storage, payments, email, security, and WhatsApp messaging.

5. How and why we use personal data

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • creating and administering accounts;
  • storing user-directed notes, files, contact details, schedules, and instructions;
  • sending check-ins, reminders, verification requests, service messages, and authorised notifications;
  • recording responses and operating the trusted-contact and nominee workflow;
  • sharing stored information with nominees when the user-selected conditions and verification process are completed;
  • processing subscriptions and payments;
  • providing support and responding to privacy or deletion requests;
  • preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorised access, and other security incidents;
  • maintaining records, enforcing our terms, resolving disputes, and complying with applicable law; and
  • monitoring reliability and improving the service using information that is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Where consent is required, users may withdraw it by changing available settings, discontinuing the relevant optional feature, requesting deletion through the application, or contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing already completed and may prevent us from providing features that depend on the withdrawn data.

6. WhatsApp and Meta

If a user or contact provides a WhatsApp number and opts in, we may use the WhatsApp Business Platform supplied by Meta to send check-ins, reminders, trusted-contact alerts, and service communications and to receive replies. We share the recipient's telephone number, approved message-template content, message parameters, and delivery instructions with Meta as necessary to provide this feature. Meta returns message identifiers, delivery information, status events, errors, and incoming replies.

WhatsApp participation is optional. A user may withdraw WhatsApp consent through available application settings or by contacting us. Where available and appropriate, we may use another configured communication method after WhatsApp is disabled or delivery fails. Meta and WhatsApp process information under their own applicable terms and privacy notices.

7. When we share personal data

We may share personal data only as reasonably necessary with:

  • User-designated recipients: trusted contacts, witnesses, nominees, or other recipients selected by the user, in accordance with the user's instructions and the service workflow.
  • Service providers: providers of cloud hosting, data storage, backups, email delivery, WhatsApp messaging, payment processing, security, and technical support. They may process data only to provide their services to us and subject to their contracts and privacy obligations.
  • Professional advisers: legal, accounting, insurance, or security advisers when reasonably necessary.
  • Authorities and other parties: when required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect people, rights, property, and the security or integrity of the service.
  • Business-successor parties: in connection with a proposed or completed reorganisation, financing, acquisition, sale, or transfer, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.

We do not sell personal data or share it for third-party behavioural advertising.

8. Payments

Payments may be processed by a third-party payment provider. The provider may collect payment-card, bank, billing, identity, fraud-prevention, and transaction information under its own privacy policy. We generally receive transaction identifiers, payment status, subscription details, and limited billing information needed to administer the service and maintain required records.

9. Hosting, storage, and international processing

We use third-party infrastructure and storage providers to host the service and protect stored files and records. These providers or their systems may be located outside the user's state or country. Where personal data is processed across borders, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate contractual, organisational, and technical safeguards and to comply with applicable transfer restrictions.

10. Data retention and deletion

We ordinarily retain a user's data while the account is active and for as long as needed to provide the requested service. After the user's stored information has been shared with the user's nominees through the final authorised workflow, we retain the related information for up to two years.

This two-year period does not apply when:

  • the user or an authorised person submits a valid earlier deletion request; or
  • the user has selected an available option for immediate deletion after the final authorised sharing or transaction is completed.

When deletion applies, we delete or anonymise personal data within a reasonable period, subject to identity and authority verification, technical backup cycles, fraud and security prevention, dispute resolution, and any legal or regulatory retention requirement. Data retained for one of these limited reasons is restricted from unrelated use and deleted when the reason ends.

Subject to applicable law and appropriate identity or authority verification, a user may request to:

  • access a summary of personal data and processing activities;
  • correct, complete, or update inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • delete personal data;
  • withdraw consent for optional processing;
  • receive information about the categories of service providers or other recipients with whom data has been shared;
  • nominate another person to exercise applicable rights where permitted by law; and
  • raise a question or grievance about our handling of personal data.

Requests may be submitted through an account-deletion feature available in the application or by emailing subrata3dx@gmail.com. Please describe the request and the account it concerns. We may ask for information needed to verify identity, authority, and account ownership. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including access controls, authentication, transport encryption, restricted storage, audit records, and service monitoring where appropriate. No online service, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorised access.

13. Children's privacy

The Final Notes is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly permit a child to create an account. If you believe a child has provided personal data without appropriate authorisation, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.
